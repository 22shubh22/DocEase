# DocEase — Modern Clinic Management, Simplified

## What is DocEase?

DocEase is a complete clinic management platform built for outpatient departments. It streamlines your OPD operations, manages patients, generates digital prescriptions, and tracks finances — all from one platform built for clinics like yours.

It replaces paper registers, scattered spreadsheets, and fragmented tools with a single, intuitive digital system that the entire clinic team can use.

---

## The Problem DocEase Solves

Most small and mid-sized clinics still run on paper. Patient records are in registers, OPD queues are managed by shouting names, prescriptions are handwritten, and there's zero visibility into how much the clinic collected today or this week.

When a follow-up patient walks in, the doctor has no quick way to pull up their last visit, see what medicines were prescribed, or check if they had any allergies. Assistants juggle between registration books and call-out lists. And at the end of the month, calculating collections is a manual, error-prone headache.

DocEase changes all of that.

## How It Works — The Complete Patient Journey

### Step 1: Register the Patient
When a patient walks into the clinic, the assistant or doctor opens DocEase and registers them in seconds. They enter the patient's name, age, gender, phone number, emergency contact, address, and blood group. If the patient has any allergies — say, a penicillin allergy — that gets recorded with a visual warning that shows up every time this patient is accessed. Medical history notes can also be added.

The system automatically generates a unique patient code like PT-0001, so every patient has a clear identity in the system. If the patient has visited before, a quick search by name, phone number, or patient code pulls up their full profile instantly.

### Step 2: Add to the OPD Queue
Once registered, the patient is added to today's OPD queue with their chief complaint — "Fever and cough," for example. The clinic can choose from pre-configured chief complaints or type in a custom one.

The OPD queue is a live dashboard that auto-refreshes every 10 seconds. It shows real-time statistics: how many patients are waiting, how many are in progress, and how many are completed. Patients move through a clear status flow: Waiting, then In Progress, then Completed. The queue can be reordered — if a priority patient needs to be seen first, just move them up.

If a patient was given a follow-up date during their last visit and that date is today, the system shows a follow-up alert so they're not forgotten. And if someone tries to add a patient who's already in today's queue, the system warns about the duplicate.

### Step 3: Consultation and Prescription
When the doctor is ready to see the patient , theyclick "Start Consultation" from the OPD queue. This opens a structured visit form where the doctor records everything:

**Vitals** — Blood pressure, temperature, pulse, weight, height, and SpO2.

**Consultation details** — The doctor selects or types in symptoms, diagnosis, clinical observations, and recommended tests. All of these come from customizable dropdowns, so the clinic can set up its most common options for quick selection while still allowing custom entries.

**Prescription** — The doctor adds medicines with dosage and duration. Multiple medicines can be added per visit. For example: "Paracetamol 500mg, twice daily, for 5 days."

**Amount** — The consultation fee or any amount collected is recorded right there in the visit.

**Follow-up** — If the patient needs to come back, the doctor schedules a follow-up date.

While writing the prescription, the doctor can see the patient's previous visit history in a side panel — what was diagnosed last time, what medicines were given, and any allergies. Allergy warnings are prominently displayed so they're impossible to miss.

Once done, the doctor saves the visit and can print a clean, formatted prescription with their digital signature. Visits can also be edited after completion if needed.

### Step 4: Track Collections and Reports
At any point, the clinic owner can go to the Collection Reports page and see exactly how much money was collected. Reports can be filtered by date range — today, this week, this month, or a custom range. They can be grouped by day, by doctor, or by date range. If the clinic has multiple doctors, the owner can filter by a specific doctor to see their collections separately.

Every visit's amount is tracked individually, so there's a complete breakdown of where the money came from. Reports can be exported or printed.

## Team Management — Everyone Has Their Role

DocEase supports multi-user access with role-based permissions. A clinic can have up to 5 additional team members beyond the owner.

**The Clinic Owner (Doctor)** has full access to everything — patients, OPD queue, visits, prescriptions, reports, clinic settings, and team management. They can add doctors and assistants, control what each person can access, and configure the entire clinic.

**Associate Doctors** can do everything clinical — manage patients, run OPD consultations, create prescriptions, and view reports. They just can't manage the team or change permissions.

**Assistants** handle the front desk — they can register patients, manage the OPD queue, and view patient histories. But they can't create visits, write prescriptions, or access financial reports. This keeps clinical work with the doctors while giving assistants the tools they need.

Each role's permissions are granular. The owner can control exactly what each team member can do: patient management (view, create, edit, delete), OPD management (view, manage), visit management (view, create, edit), and settings management. It's flexible enough to match any clinic's workflow.

## Clinic Customization — Make It Yours

Every clinic is different. A general physician sees different complaints than a pediatrician. A dermatologist prescribes different medicines than an orthopedic surgeon.

DocEase lets the clinic owner customize all the dropdown options used during consultations:
- **Chief Complaints** — "Fever," "Cough," "Skin Rash," or whatever your clinic commonly sees
- **Symptoms** — tailored to your specialty
- **Diagnosis options** — your most common diagnoses for quick selection
- **Clinical Observations** — standard observations you record
- **Test recommendations** — common tests you order
- **Medicine names** — your frequently prescribed medicines
- **Dosage options** — "500mg," "1 tablet," "5ml," etc.
- **Duration options** — "3 days," "1 week," "1 month," etc.

Options can be reordered, activated, or deactivated without deleting them. Print settings can also be customized so prescriptions print exactly the way the clinic wants.
